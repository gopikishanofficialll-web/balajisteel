About The Foundry

Built on Steel. Backed by a Name You Can Trust.

Balaji Steel & Railing was founded on a simple principle: premium materials, honest fabrication, and installations that are built to last a lifetime — not a season. Every railing, gate and grill that leaves our workshop is measured, welded and polished to a standard fit for the most demanding residences and commercial façades.

We work exclusively with corrosion-resistant SS 304 and SS 316 grade steel, toughened safety glass, and precision CNC laser-cutting — so what we deliver today still looks and performs like new, years from now.

What We Craft

Our Premium Services

From residential balustrades to commercial-grade gates, each service is executed with the same obsessive precision and premium-grade material sourcing.

01

Stainless Steel Railing

Balcony, staircase and terrace railings in SS 304/316 — hand-polished to a mirror or satin finish, engineered for structural safety and lasting shine.

02

Toughened Glass Railing

Frameless and semi-framed glass balustrade systems using 10–12mm toughened glass, paired with steel or SS fittings for a seamless, modern silhouette.

03

Designer CNC Main Gates

Laser-cut, custom-designed main gates that merge intricate detailing with heavy-duty steel construction — a true architectural statement piece.

04

Safety Window Grills

Elegant yet secure window grill systems, custom-fabricated to match your façade design without compromising on safety or ventilation.
